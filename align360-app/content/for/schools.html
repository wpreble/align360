The Problem

The ceiling every counseling office hits.

Not a staffing problem alone. A system that reacts to confusion instead of preventing it.

For the institution

  • 400 students per counselor makes real depth impossible
  • Career services react to confusion instead of preventing it
  • No tool reaches students before they arrive on campus
  • Alumni relationship ends the day they walk across the stage
  • No institutional data on where curriculum and wiring mismatch
  • Hard to attract students who don't know what they want yet

For the student

  • No system connects classroom choices to real-world fit
  • Support ends at graduation — no ongoing thread
  • AI anxiety with no language for what they uniquely bring
