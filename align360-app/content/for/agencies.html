The Problem

The ceiling every placement desk hits.

Not a sourcing problem. A system that can't see wiring — only resumes and interviews.

For the agency

  • Placement decisions rely on resume and interview — wiring is invisible
  • No ongoing relationship with candidates after placement
  • No proprietary methodology to differentiate from competitors
  • No early relationship with talent before they're on the market
  • Candidate database has no intelligence layer — it's just records
  • Can't answer what a candidate does that AI cannot

For the candidate

  • Judged on paper, not on how they actually think and decide
  • Relationship ends the moment the offer is signed
  • No system helps them understand their own fit before applying
  • No language for their edge in an AI-disrupted market
  • No connection back to the agency for their next move
  • Placed once, then forgotten until they resurface on a job board
