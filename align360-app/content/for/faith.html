The Problem

The ceiling every discipleship pipeline hits.

Not a commitment problem. A system that resets every conversation instead of building on the last one.

For the church

  • Small group conversations reset every session — no persistent model of the member
  • Staff burnout treated as a spiritual problem when it's often structural misalignment
  • No ongoing connection when members move through life transitions
  • No mechanism to fund ministry beyond tithes and donations

For the member

  • Believes they have purpose, but has no practical system for living from it
  • Identity shaped by culture and pressure — not by design
  • Gifts described in theological language, not lived, specific, actionable terms
  • Loses connection to the church during exactly the seasons they need it most

The Arc
