Three domains · Nine questions · Two minutes daily
Biological
Sleep quality, energy, physical readiness, body signals. How your body is showing up today.
Psychological
Mental clarity, focus, emotional stability, cognitive bandwidth. How your mind is showing up today.
Spiritual
Sense of purpose, groundedness, connection to what matters. How your spirit is showing up today.
What B3 produces
A Day
All three domains strong. Push toward high-stakes work, important conversations, creative output, and decisions that matter.
B Day
Two of three domains strong. Normal productive day. Protect one domain from additional load. Good for execution and collaboration.
C Day
One or more domains significantly depleted. Protect, restore, and do only what only you can do. Do not make irreversible decisions today.
After 21 days of check-ins, Align builds your personal Work Rhythm Profile — when your A Days cluster, when your C Days tend to appear, which domain is most stable and which is most variable. The profile makes the pattern visible. You stop being surprised by yourself.
What changes when you know

The Work Rhythm Profile shows you that Tuesday mornings are historically your strongest cognitive window. You stop scheduling meetings there. You start protecting it for your best work.
The people around you understand you better
When you share your B3 state with a coach or a team lead (you choose what to share), they stop interpreting your low days as disengagement. They understand it as a domain signal with a specific intervention. The relationship changes.
