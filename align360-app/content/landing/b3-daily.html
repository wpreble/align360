What Pulse360™ Produces

One of three state classifications. Every morning.

Each domain is scored from your nine answers. The three domain scores combine into your state for today. The state tells you — and your AI companion — exactly how to approach the next 24 hours.

All three domains strong
Peak
Push toward high-stakes work, important conversations, creative output, and decisions that matter. This is your leverage window. Master Chief™ knows it and opens your session accordingly.
Two of three domains strong
Steady
Normal productive day. One domain has friction — Align notes it without over-indexing on it. Good for execution, collaboration, and follow-through. Protect the one domain that's lower.
One or more domains depleted
Recovery
Protect, restore, and do only what only you can do. Do not make irreversible decisions today. Master Chief™ adjusts — high-stakes prompts are suppressed and the session is restorative.
CUSTOM LABELS
Peak / Steady / Recovery are the defaults. After 7 consecutive check-ins, you can rename each state in your own language. Some people call them On Fire / Flowing / Renewing. Some call them Full / Moderate / Rest. The scoring never changes. The label is yours.

    Every Master Chief™ session loads your Pulse360™ state before you say a word. On Peak days, it opens with leverage questions. On Recovery days, it suppresses high-stakes prompts and adjusts everything. The platform's intelligence about you compounds daily — not session to session, but day to day.
